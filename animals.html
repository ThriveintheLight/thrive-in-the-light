The Technology

How LifeWave Patches Work on Animals

LifeWave's patches contain no drugs and nothing is absorbed into the body. They work through a simple principle used by nature itself: light.

Your animal's body heat causes the patch to reflect specific wavelengths of light back into the tissue beneath it — the same principle by which sunlight triggers vitamin D production in skin. When placed on specific acupressure points, this gentle stimulation encourages the body's natural energy flow, supporting comfort and recovery from the inside out.

Safe for competition? AcuLife patches contain no prohibited substances and have been used by competitive equine athletes. Always check your specific governing body's rules. Patches are removed before any drug testing as a matter of best practice.
