About AcuLife

A New Kind of Care for Your Horse

You know your horse. You know when something's off — when they're not moving freely, not performing at their best, or simply not themselves.

LifeWave's AcuLife® patches bring the same light-therapy science behind the popular X39 human patches to equine wellness. Placed on acupressure points on your horse's body, these small self-adhesive patches use reflected light to gently stimulate the body's natural energy pathways — with no drugs, no needles, and no veterinary prescription required.

AcuLife is recognised as a valid modality by both the American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA) and the American Association of Equine Practitioners (AAEP).

The Technology

How AcuLife Patches Work

LifeWave's patches contain no drugs and nothing is absorbed into the body. Instead, they work through a principle used by nature itself: light.

1

Light Interacts with the Patch

Your horse's body heat causes the patch to reflect specific wavelengths of light back into the tissue beneath it — the same principle by which sunlight triggers vitamin D production in skin.

2

Acupressure Points Are Stimulated

The patches are placed on specific acupuncture/acupressure points on your horse's body — the same points used in traditional equine acupuncture for thousands of years.

3

Energy Balance Is Restored

Stimulating these points encourages the natural flow of energy through the body, reducing tension, supporting healthy inflammation response, and soothing discomfort.

4

Results Within Minutes

Many horse owners report noticeable changes in movement and comfort within minutes of application. Each patch is effective for up to 12 hours.

Application Guide

How to Apply the Patches

Each AcuLife kit includes a White patch, a Tan patch with bead, and placement guidance. No veterinary training required — anyone can apply them.

Patch Placement

White patch → Right side of horse Tan patch (with bead) → Left side of horse

Patches are placed on acupressure points along the neck, back, and hindquarters. Key points include:

  • Bladder 10 — base of neck
  • Large Intestine 16 — shoulder
  • Bladder 13 — mid back
  • Bladder 23 — lower back
  • Bladder 28 — hindquarters
  • Additional points as needed

⚠️ Safety note: Always stand to one side of your horse when applying patches to avoid injury from kicking. It is recommended to have experience handling horses before applying.
