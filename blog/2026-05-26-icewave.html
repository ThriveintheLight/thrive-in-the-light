What Is IceWave™?

IceWave™ is a phototherapy patch system developed by Lifewave — a company with over 20 years of research into non-invasive wellness technology. Unlike creams, pills, or other topical products, IceWave™ uses the body's own light energy to support comfort. It comes as a two-patch system: a white patch and a tan patch, each placed on the skin near the area of concern. No drugs. No chemicals. Nothing entering your body.

The Science Behind It

Lifewave's phototherapy technology is built on the principle that the human body emits low-level infrared light. The IceWave™ patches are engineered to reflect specific wavelengths of that light back into the skin, designed to support the body's natural processes around comfort and recovery. This approach is non-transdermal — meaning nothing from the patch crosses the skin barrier.
