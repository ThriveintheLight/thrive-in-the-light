What Is X49™?

X49™ is a phototherapy patch developed by Lifewave that may support physical performance, cardiovascular health, and body composition. Like all Lifewave patches, X49™ contains no drugs and nothing enters the body through the skin. Instead, it works by interacting with your body's own natural light energy — supporting your body's natural processes for strength and recovery.

The Science Behind It

Lifewave's technology is rooted in the science of photobiomodulation — the idea that specific wavelengths of light can influence cellular function. X49™ is engineered to reflect these wavelengths back into the body, encouraging natural performance-enhancing processes. In a clinical study involving men aged 40–81, X49™ may support exercise performance, offering encouragement to those who want to stay active as they age.
