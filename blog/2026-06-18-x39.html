You've probably heard about stem cells. Maybe you've seen headlines about cutting-edge therapies or wondered if any of it applies to everyday wellness. But here's a question worth sitting with: what if your body already has the ability to renew and restore itself — and what if there were a simple, drug-free way to support that process?

That's the idea behind Lifewave's X39® phototherapy patch.

What Is X39®?

X39® is a wearable phototherapy patch that is placed on the skin — no needles, no drugs, nothing entering your body. It works by interacting with your body's own natural light energy, using a patented technology designed to support the elevation of GHK-Cu, a naturally occurring peptide in the body. GHK-Cu has been studied for its association with healthy cell function and tissue support.

In simpler terms: X39® may help your body do more of what it already knows how to do.

The Science Behind It

Lifewave's phototherapy technology is built on decades of research. The X39® patch reflects specific wavelengths of light from your own body heat, which may interact with biological pathways related to GHK-Cu peptide activity.

GHK-Cu (copper peptide) has been the subject of substantial scientific interest. Studies have explored its potential role in supporting tissue health, skin regeneration, and even energy metabolism. Lifewave's X39® is supported by more than 70 clinical and pre-clinical studies examining the effects of phototherapy on the body's natural systems.

Whether you're an athlete, an active adult, or someone managing the physical demands of daily life, X39® may support recovery after exertion. Many users pair it with X49® for an added layer of performance and stamina support.
