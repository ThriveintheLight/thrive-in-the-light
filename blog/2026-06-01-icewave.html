What Is IceWave™?

The Science Behind the Patches

Lifewave's phototherapy technology is based on the principle that the body emits and responds to light. The IceWave™ patches are designed to reflect specific wavelengths of the body's own infrared light, which may support the body's natural signaling processes.

This isn't a new concept — phototherapy has been studied for decades. What makes Lifewave unique is the patent-protected way the patches work as a paired system, with the white and tan patches each supporting different aspects of the body's natural response.
